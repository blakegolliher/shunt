# H3 acceptance: resumable membership and observed health

H3 (ADR-0021 D4: H3a–H3e) makes a control-plane membership change a durable operation and a
member's health something observed rather than inferred. It does not touch the request path: no
proxy, admission or upstream code changed, so the data-path benchmarks of docs/bench/h2.md stand.
The acceptance is T12 and T13 on real etcd, through the API, both CLIs, the Control plane screen and
`make fleet`'s real processes. The health sampler's own cost at T19's 30 members is one cycle of
about 0.4 ms every 5 s, and a status read costs 26 µs and probes nothing. Measured 2026-09-28 on
the H3e working tree over `53f5b73`.

## Method

- Host: AMD EPYC 7402P (24 cores, 16 visible), Linux 5.14.0-570.17.1.el9_6.x86_64, Go 1.27.1,
  `GOMAXPROCS` 16, with no fleet, demo or other shunt process running.
- Unit and real-etcd tests: `make all` (lint, vet, test, `-race`, fuzz) and `make ui-test`.
- End to end: `make fleet`, run alone (three `shunt-control` processes and two proxies over the e2e
  Garage and MinIO).
- Benchmarks: `go test ./internal/cp -run '^$' -bench BenchmarkHealth -benchmem -count 6`. etcd's
  log lines interleave with the result lines, so the medians are read from the raw file,
  `test/bench/h3/health.txt`.

(H3b), the peer-URL reconciliation dropped (H3b), the removal's token bound to the ID alone and the

## T13: health is observed, never inferred

| T13 requirement | Where it is shown |
|---|---|
| A named member stopped is never healthy | `cp.TestHealthObservesEachMember` (connection refused); real etcd: `cp.TestHealthOnARealCluster` (c3, then c2, stopped) |
| Quorum partitioned; bounded, fresh evidence; partial status available | `cp.TestHealthQuorumUnavailable`; `cp.TestHealthOnARealCluster` (status answers without quorum, `partial`, `has_quorum` false); `make fleet` step 3: with c2 and c3 down, `shunt-control status` answers from c1's view, partial, quorum `unavailable` with its code, c2 and c3 unreachable (and c1, whose etcd then has no leader in view), exit 1 |
| Probe answers delayed or dropped | `cp.TestHealthCycleIsBoundedAndViewerIndependent`: a member that never answers is unreachable at the cycle's 3 s deadline and the cycle returns then; at most five probes at once |
| A learner that never started | Unknown with its reason, never healthy: `cp.TestHealthObservesEachMember`; real processes: `make fleet` lists c3's learner as a learner, not started and unknown before its node runs, and `status` exits 3 |
| Stale observations | Every observation, and quorum's, older than 15 s reads unknown: `cp.TestHealthObservationsGoStale` |
| Many browser viewers | A thousand status reads send no probe: `cp.TestHealthCycleIsBoundedAndViewerIndependent`; the screen reads the samples the status carries |
| The screen | "shows each control member's observed health, never health from a name, and quorum as observed" |

## The sampler's cost

With this node and 29 peers whose probes answer at once (the network apart), six samples each:

| Benchmark | Median | Memory | Allocations |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cp.HealthCycle30`: one cycle, 29 probes five at a time, and a linearizable read through the node's real etcd member | 387 µs (349–440) | 36.6 KB | 587 |
| `cp.HealthSnapshot30`: health for one status read (the local membership and 30 observations) | 25.5 µs (25.0–26.4) | 10.1 KB | 163 |

A cycle runs every 5 s (plus up to 1 s of jitter) whatever the number of viewers, so the sampler
costs a node under 0.01 % of a core at 30 members, and the probes it sends are 29 small GETs per
cycle, about five a second. Real probes add their network round trips, but the cycle bounds them: five in
flight, all within 3 s. A status read costs the same with one viewer or a thousand, and sends
nothing.

## End to end

`make fleet` (H3e) forms the cluster with one node joined each way and cancels a join whose node
never started, then runs the unchanged steps 2–7 (quorum loss under 256 workers, a cache restart,
silent and crashed proxies, a control node killed while holding a barrier). The status checks read
observed health: formation waits for three healthy voters and quorum reachable, and quorum loss is
seen in `status` itself, as above. Of the four runs on the H3e tree, one stopped on step 2's
telemetry cross-check (fleet p99 11.3 % from the client's, limit 10 %, 0 errors in 50,548
operations), the load-sensitive check STATUS records, on a host whose load average was 5.4 from
other workloads; the others measured 4.6–8.2 %. One stopped on a check of this slice that expected
c1 healthy without quorum, which H3d's rule rightly calls unreachable (no leader in view); the check
was corrected. The last run passed every step.

## Open

- `shunt-control operation show|wait|resume|cancel`, sharing one operation client with `shunt
  operation` (contracts §2), is T17's and moves to H5. A join's cancel and resume are `shunt
  operation cancel|resume` meanwhile, and the Control plane screen offers both.
- T19 measures health at 30 members in-process (above). Thirty real control nodes were not run: etcd
  advises no more than seven members, and shunt's how-to forms three or five.
- A manual pass of the join wizard and removal on `make demo-ui` is the operator's, as in H0 and H2.
