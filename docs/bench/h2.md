# H2 benchmarks and acceptance: drain barriers, leases, incarnations

H2 (ADR-0021 D2: H2a–H2f) left the data path's timing unchanged: with twelve samples a side, no
request-path benchmark moved significantly (geomean +0.44 %). A resigned GET and a PUT each make one
more allocation (240 → 241, 179 → 180). A healthy barrier on a two-proxy, three-control-node fleet
with 1 s heartbeats commits in 1.0 s at the median and 2.0 s at p99, against a 5 s target. H2f's
per-heartbeat fixes cut a control node's heartbeat at 100,000 placements from 259 ms to 1.2 ms and a
member's from 563 ms to 31 ms. Two costs at the design's 1,000-proxy scale remain and are carried
(below). Measured 2026-09-25.

## Method

- Host: AMD EPYC 7402P (24 cores, 16 visible), Linux 5.14.0-570.17.1.el9_6.x86_64, Go 1.27.1,
  `GOMAXPROCS` 16. Every benchmark ran on its own, one after another, with no demo fleet, e2e run
  or other shunt process running (the e2e MinIO and Garage containers were up and idle).
- Before H2: `32819a2` (H1f, the last commit before H2a), in a separate worktree. Before H2f's
  heartbeat fixes: `e0bd1fd` (H2e plus the review fixes) with the new benchmark files copied in.
  After: the H2f working tree on `8ffeae7`.
- `go test -run '^$' -bench <set> -benchmem -benchtime 1s -count 6`, compared with benchstat at
  the Makefile's pinned version; the request-path rerun used `-benchtime 2s -count 12`. The etcd
  fleet benchmarks ran `-count 3` (2000 iterations; 20 for the full-table reads), and etcd's log
  lines interleave with their result lines, so their medians are read from the raw files.
- Raw output and comparisons: `test/bench/h2/` (`datapath-*`, `requestpath-*`, `filecopy-*`,
  `control-plane-*`).

## Data path: before and after H2

The first `-count 6` pass over `internal/proxy`, `internal/sigv4/...`, `internal/s3/...`,
`internal/upstream`, `internal/migrate` and `internal/directory` showed `proxy.ResignSmallGET` at
+14.3 % (p=0.026) with ±27 % and ±23 % spreads. The request-path set was run again with twelve
samples a side (`requestpath-benchstat.txt`):

| Benchmark | Before (`32819a2`) | After (H2f) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| `proxy.SmallGET` | 343.9 µs ± 4 %, 107 allocs | 366.2 µs ± 9 %, 107 allocs | ~ (p=0.160) |
| `proxy.PUT1MiB` | 1.945 ms ± 7 %, 179 allocs | 1.908 ms ± 6 %, 180 allocs | ~ (p=0.319) |
| `proxy.ResignSmallGET` | 487.5 µs ± 9 %, 240 allocs | 481.5 µs ± 7 %, 241 allocs | ~ (p=0.319) |
| `proxy.ResignSignedChunkPUT1MiB` | 5.008 ms ± 3 %, 4,726 allocs | 4.940 ms ± 6 %, 4,729 allocs | ~ (p=0.630) |

The +14 % was noise. The extra allocation per mutation is the context a mutation's upstream request
now runs on, detached from the client's (ADR-0021, decisions of 2026-09-25: a mutation sent whole
runs to the backend's answer). Of the rest of the first pass, `directory.Stamp` allocates 10 % more
(the barrier fields a write now carries; the write path, not the request path) and
`directory.Lookup` reads +24 % at ±28 % spread (p=0.015; 38.6 → 48.0 ns, nothing in H2 touched it).

`proxy.MigratingDeleteObjects1000Keys` is not compared: the fake backend's DeleteObjects changed with
the spread-bucket DeleteObjects fix of the same day (it now checks `Content-MD5`, which the benchmark
now sends). After: 14.4 ms, 2.25 MiB,
37,066 allocs for a 1,000-key body sent to both clusters (`datapath-h2-deleteobjects.txt`).

## Unrelated buckets while one is held

`proxy.ResignSmallGETUnderHold` and `ResignSmallPUTUnderHold` time a request to bucket X while bucket
Y's mutations gate is closed, with and without an uncertain outcome counted on Y, and in a directory
of 10,000 placements with 100 held. X's allocations are the same in every variant; a mutation
refused at a closed gate costs 93 ns with sixteen goroutines at it (`admission.EnterClosedParallel`).
Nothing on the request path scans the directory or waits on another bucket's gate.

## A healthy barrier, and a barrier behind an upload

Section 7 of `make fleet` (three control nodes, two proxies, `heartbeat_interval: 1s`) times 40
barrier operations, read-only on and off, each a hold, every proxy's drain and a commit: **p50
1,037 ms, p99 2,036 ms, max 2,036 ms**, against the proposed 5 s target. The same section starts a
512 MiB PutObject through one proxy and then turns read-only on: the barrier took 3,794 ms,
waiting for the upload to finish rather than cutting it off.

## The per-heartbeat directory copies (H2f)

`Snapshot.File()` deep-copies the whole directory: 127 ms and 66 MiB at 100,000 placements
(`directory.SnapshotFile100k`). The heartbeat handler, the barrier poll, the fence view and a
member's heartbeat each made one or more such copies, mostly to read the directory's identity.
H2f reads it (and the secret generations and moving buckets) from the snapshot without copying:

| Benchmark | Before (`e0bd1fd`) | After (H2f) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| `control.HeartbeatHandler`, 100,000 placements | 258.8 ms, 126 MiB | 1.20 ms, 695 KiB | −99.5 % |
| `control.HeartbeatHandler`, 10 placements | 2.00 ms | 1.87 ms | ~ |
| `control.DrainBlockers1kDir100k` (one barrier, 1,000 members) | 130.5 ms, 63 MiB | 0.70 ms, 296 KiB | −99.5 % |
| `control.FenceStatus1k` (placement view, 1,000 members) | 1.158 s, 648 MiB | 0.36 ms, 296 KiB | −99.97 % |
| `member.Beat`, 1,000 placements | 7.52 ms | 1.55 ms | −79 % |
| `member.Beat`, 100,000 placements | 563 ms, 252 MiB | 31.5 ms, 2.4 MiB | −94 % |

`member.Beat` at 100,000 placements still reads every placement once for the moving buckets'
fallback counts (31 ms); that scan is proportional to the directory and off the request path.

Medians of six runs on the H2f tree (`control-plane-*.txt`).

| Benchmark | What it is | Time |
|---|---|---|
| `admission.EnterRelease` | a mutation's token, sixteen goroutines on one placement | 136 ns |
| `admission.EnterReleaseSpread` | the same over 10,000 placements | 8.2 ns |
| `admission.Acks` | a heartbeat's drain proof over 10,000 placements, uncached | 5.35 ms |
| `admission.AcksSnapshot` | the same, the snapshot's barriers derived once | 2.1 µs |
| `admission.Barriers100kFirst` / `Cached` | barrier derivation, 100,000 placements, 100 held | 28.0 ms / 15 ns |

| `control.DrainBlockers1kDrained` / `Mixed` | one barrier, 1,000 members, 100 acks each; mixed has 100 silent or unresolved | 1.01 ms / 1.07 ms |
| `member.HeartbeatMarshal` / `Unmarshal` | 100 acks and two clusters' telemetry | 521 µs / 1.79 ms |
| `member.CapTelemetryKept` / `Dropped` | the size check at the cap | 2.31 ms / 2.37 ms |
| `member.DirectoryDecode100k` / `CacheDecode100k` | a fetched 100,000-placement directory; the restart cache | 435 ms / 424 ms |
| `member.Install100k` | one version of 100,000 placements installed | 758 ms |
| `upstream.RegistryRotateSecret` | one cluster's secret rotated (16 clusters) | 50.8 µs |
| `cp.FleetHeartbeat1k` / `Telemetry` | one heartbeat applied in etcd, 1,000 members | 5.10 ms / 6.56 ms |
| `cp.FleetHeartbeat1kParallel` | heartbeats applied concurrently, per heartbeat | 1.03–1.14 ms |

**Heartbeat size.** An acknowledgement is 153 bytes. A heartbeat with 100 acknowledgements is 15.7
KiB of safety fields; two clusters of telemetry add 173 KiB (192.7 KB in all). 4,096
acknowledgements with no telemetry are 627 KB, under the 983,040-byte cap. The densest telemetry
window that fits is 972 KB (11 clusters, 264 sketches); the next denser one is dropped, and the
acknowledgements are kept. Since H2f the size check counts the moving buckets' fallback counts too
(`TestHeartbeatCapCountsFallbackReads`).

## Carried to T19 (1,000 proxies)

Both are measured at the design's scale, not seen at the fleet sizes run so far, and neither is on
the request path.

- **A barrier poll reads the whole fleet table.** `drainBlockers` reads every member's record, whose
  last heartbeat includes its telemetry: 216 ms and 15.9 MB per poll at 1,000 members without
  telemetry, 841 ms and 193 MB with two clusters of it (`cp.FleetMembers1k*`), against a 100 ms
  poll. The evaluation itself is 1 ms. The fix is to keep telemetry out of the record a barrier
  reads, or a watched cache of the fleet on each control node.
- **Heartbeat apply throughput.** About 900–970 heartbeats a second on single-node etcd
  (`cp.FleetHeartbeat1kParallel`), just under the 1,000 a second that 1,000 proxies on a 1 s
  interval send.

## Acceptance runs on the H2f build

- `make walkthrough`: green, 0 errors in 59,784 verify operations. Before H2f it stopped at
  `ramp 1.0` on `backend_outcome_unknown` (a client disconnect counted as an uncertain outcome) and,
  once past that, failed verify with 503s for the whole cutover window.
- `make fleet`: green, seven sections: the ramp under an alternating writer; 256 workers on an ACTIVE
  bucket with quorum lost, 0 errors in 12,081 operations; a restart with the control plane down; a
  silent proxy blocking steps, cancel, forget refused, an owner crash resumed on another node, a
  clean retirement, a crash resolved with an attestation; mover, cutover and purge; and the barrier
  latency above.
- `make soak` (`TestFleetSoak`, T14): 10 m 15 s, 47 cycles, 235 fenced steps, 47 partitions, 47
  owner crashes each resumed on the other control node, 1,284,994 client operations (543,821 writes
  retried after a 503), **0 violations**, seed 1790348691328442888. Goroutines 88 warm, 93 after
  recovery (no trend across cycles); retained bundles 0 and transports 2 per proxy throughout. The
  negative control, `TestFleetWithoutTheFence`, finds its violations (21 in the soak agent's run).
- `make property`: 763,658 operations in 2 m, 0 violations, 0 lost writes.
- `make test`, `make race`, `make lint` (0 issues), `make fuzz` (13 targets, 30 s each) and
  `FuzzParseDeleteResult` (30 s), `make ui-lint`, `make ui-test` (54 tests), `tsc --noEmit`: green.
- Manual UI pass on `make demo-ui` (0.0.0.0): pending → succeeded; blocked on a stopped proxy, then
  cancelled; the stopped proxy shown silent; owner lost when control node c2 was killed, resumed on
  c1, and succeeded once the proxy came back; keyboard use and error focus.
